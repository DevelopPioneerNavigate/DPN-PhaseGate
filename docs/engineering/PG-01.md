# PG-01 Paired Evidence Stations (concept, not hardware build)
**Maturity:** Paper concept. **Purpose:** instrument *claims* rigorously, not generate physical teleportation.
## Proposed modules
- A and B: inspectable enclosures with independent measurement, low-voltage indicator and tamper detection.
- Mass measurement: load cell plus ADC with reference-mass calibration.
- Independent video: more than one view of each station and clock markers.
- Environment: optional temperature/humidity to quantify sensor drift.
- Local timestamp aggregator: UTC logs and clock-offset checks; no automated claims classifier.
## Safe baseline demonstration
Record a tagged inert object at station A, with empty station B. Run a sham activation and track readings. **Expected:** no transfer. Investigate any non-null reading as possible calibration, data, hardware or human-process error before proposing any new physics.
## Future parts decision
Document component specs, costs, supplier, schematic, operating voltage, battery/electrical safety, mechanical safeguards, calibration tolerances and limits. No parts are procured by this repo.
## Explicit scope exclusion
No improvised HV, arcs, ionizing radiation, dangerous RF/microwave exposure, high-power lasers, pressurized/cryogenic hardware, explosive or hazardous chemical experiments, or subject testing. Specialized work requires expert safety/regulatory review.
