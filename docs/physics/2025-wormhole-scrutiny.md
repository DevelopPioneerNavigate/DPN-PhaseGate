**PhaseGate review:** 2026-10-22. **Scientific scope:** theoretical and quantum-information interpretations, *not a constructed spacetime portal*.

## The paper and what it measured
[SRC-005, Jafferis et al., Nature 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05424-3) used a nine-qubit model on Google's Sycamore processor to measure dynamics inspired by a holographic gravitational analogue. The processor did not create a physical hole in surrounding spacetime. Describing the experiment as transporting a macroscopic object is incorrect.

## Correction, April 2025
[SRC-007](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08788-4) clarified the Fig. 3b caption concerning averaging over fermions. This correction does not independently validate physical traversability.

## Critical exchange, July 2025
- [SRC-008, Kobrin, Schuster & Yao](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08939-7) argue that the small commuting model lacks features associated with a gravitational interpretation.
- [SRC-009, Jafferis et al. reply](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08995-z) argue that the relevant **two-sided teleportation process** can merit a wormhole-style interpretation, without every process in the Hamiltonian having a gravitational description.

This is a **real methodological disagreement** about interpreting a specialized quantum experiment. Neither side demonstrates a physical opening that can accept and transmit intact ordinary objects. We should not treat an experimental analogue as a physical gateway, even if scientific arguments over the analogue evolve.

## Research tasks
1. Read the full original paper, correction, critique, and response; collect exact model assumptions and observables.
2. Distinguish quantum state evolution, holographic interpretation, and literal material transport.
3. Assess whether any approach gives a **physical macroscopic** aperture with predicted observables in our environment. If not, do not approve hardware designs premised on that interpretation.
4. Track follow-on responses, citations, methodological corrections and independent replication, with retrieval dates in the source register.

**Takeaway:** studying these papers may improve our theoretical understanding; it is not a build blueprint for the physical PhaseGate objective.
