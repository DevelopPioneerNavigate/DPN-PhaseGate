# Physics baseline | Reviewed 2026-10-22
## Verified science
**Quantum teleportation** transfers a quantum state using entanglement and classical communications; it does not send a macroscopic physical object. Source IDs SRC-001, SRC-002.
## Theoretical physics, not engineering
Traversable wormhole descriptions exist under particular mathematical models/assumptions. No physical traversable wormhole is known to have been observed, formed or stabilized. SRC-003, SRC-004.
## Analogue/simulation, not a spacetime rupture
2022 Sycamore experiments measured wormhole-like quantum information dynamics in a model. Researchers expressly state this did not create a physical spacetime rupture. SRC-005, SRC-006.
## Not demonstrated
Matter appearing across disconnected stations as an intact traversing object; a device that opens a usable two-way spacetime corridor; a practical method to engineer the required spacetime geometry.
## Claim-level taxonomy
L0 speculative concept; L1 theoretical calculation; L2 computational/quantum analogue; L3 controlled lab effect; L4 independent replicated physics; L5 independently validated macroscopic object transfer. A scientific paper about L1–L2 must not be cited as support for L5.
## Good scientific language
"Not established" is not "impossible forever." Conversely, a theoretical solution is not a construction plan. New evidence must be scoped to what measurements actually show.
