## Start here: what has actually been shown?

<img src="../../assets/phasegate-evidence-ladder.svg" width="100%" alt="Evidence levels from concept to verified physical object transfer"/>

| Scientific area | Evidence | Why it matters | The limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quantum-state teleportation** | Established state/information transfer | Proves quantum information protocols work | Does **not** transport the original macroscopic object |
| **Wormhole theory** | Particular mathematical solutions under assumptions | Examines geometry and traversability in theory | Does not demonstrate a physical portal |
| **Quantum-processor analogue** | Measured information dynamics in constrained models | Tests portions of a theoretical framework | Not a rupture of ambient spacetime |
| **Intact physical-object crossing** | **No verified result** | The PhaseGate north-star question | Mechanism, apparatus, evidence absent |
