# Feasibility matrix

**[Physics guide](PHYSICS-COMMAND.md) · [Engineering boundaries](../engineering/PG-01-architecture.md)**

<img src="../../assets/phasegate-evidence-ladder.svg" width="100%" alt="Feasibility is not a prototype recipe"/>

> **Visual interpretation:** Each approach below should be judged by the kind of evidence it produces. Mathematical or information transfer methods do not establish a buildable physical aperture.

---

| Approach | Demonstrated result | Open barrier to intact object transfer | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantum teleportation | Transfer of quantum states/information | No transport of original macroscopic matter; no gate | Read and model |
| Wormhole theoretical solutions | Particular geometries in gravity theories | No demonstrated physical formation/stability/accessibility | Research theory only |
| Quantum-processor wormhole analogue | Qubit information dynamics | Simulation of dynamics, not a real-space passage | Study interpretation |
| Optical/digital gateway | Images, sensor measurements and messages | No matter crosses | Optional *separately labeled* display |
| Conventional delivery mechanism | Physical transport through detectable route | Does not satisfy portal objective | Control condition |
| Unspecified 'energy vortex' | No identified reproducible mechanism | Non-falsifiable and possible hazardous apparatus | Reject without theory and review |

**Stop/go rule:** no investment in speculative high-energy hardware without a quantitatively falsifiable, expert-reviewed mechanism, defined observables, energy accounting and approved safety analysis.
