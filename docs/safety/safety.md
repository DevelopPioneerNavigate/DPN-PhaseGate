# PG laboratory safety policy

**[Safety command](SAFETY-COMMAND.md) · [Risk register](risks.md) · [Visual Atlas](../VISUAL-ATLAS.md)**

<img src="../../assets/phasegate-safety-gates.svg" width="100%" alt="Risk review comes before any live experiment"/>

> **Visual guide:** The diagram is a process requirement, not an approval. Qualified people must actually inspect and sign off on any permitted low-voltage bench activity.

---

**Default: document, model and measure at low voltage.** The repository does not authorize experimental hazardous equipment or human/animal tests.
Allowed initial activities: literature, simulation, non-hazardous low-voltage calibration and passive camera/scale measurements with approved supplies.
Requires independent qualified safety/regulatory review: high voltage, high-power light/lasers, RF emitters, strong magnets, pressurized/vacuum systems, cryogens, hazardous chemicals, moving machinery, radiation sources.
Stop on heat, smoke, exposed conductors, instability, unsafe pressure or unknown hazards. Record incidents and near misses, including failed/aborted protocols. Investigate environmental and human error explanations before unusual physics claims. Experiments require real documented review; GitHub issue creation is not approval.
