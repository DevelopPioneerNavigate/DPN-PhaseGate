## What may be considered

| Level | Current posture | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Research and modeling | Normal project scope | Literature, code, diagramming, modeling |
| Ordinary low-voltage instrumentation | **Requires design review before building/testing** | Camera, commercial rated supply, calibrated scale |
| Specialized or high-energy work | **Not authorized** | High voltage, radiation, hazardous lasers/RF, cryogenic/pressure equipment |
| Testing living beings or teleportation | **Not authorized** | Humans, animals or biological subjects |

## Before a real experiment

1. Define apparatus and energy hazards precisely.
2. Document risk controls, fault handling, disconnect and emergency stop.
3. Identify the real responsible owner and independent reviewer.
4. Obtain and retain qualified approval; a GitHub issue or green CI is **not** permission.
5. Follow the proposed protocol, record issues and abort when safety conditions fail.
