## North-star outcome
The *same* independently marked macroscopic object physically enters gateway A and appears at isolated gateway B, then the reverse, without ordinary transit, duplication, substitution, or concealed mechanical movement. This goal has **no experimentally demonstrated method** as of this review and must not be presented as feasible on a schedule.
## Verification requirements
- Prove unique object identity, physical integrity, spatial separation, and chronology.
- Exclude ordinary routes, duplicates, tampering and selective video/sensor reporting.
- Account for mass/energy, instrument uncertainty and classical communications.
- Use blinded sham controls, independent witnesses, robust preregistration and external replication.
- Do not infer a new physical law from an unexplained reading without investigating calibration, fraud and artifacts.
## Distinguish research products
A **physics hypothesis** attempts a falsifiable explanation of macroscopic transfer; a **simulation** models dynamics; a **measurement rig** logs observations; an **optical/digital portal** transmits images/data. None of the latter three is evidence that an object traversed spacetime.
## Advancement gates
Literature -> falsifiable predictions -> independent theoretical review -> safe instrumentation -> preregistered trial with controls -> critical replication -> possible published scientific claim. No claims or experimental authorization are implied by passing a software gate.
