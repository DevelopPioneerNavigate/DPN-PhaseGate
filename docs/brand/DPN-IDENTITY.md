## Design language
**Theme:** midnight engineering laboratory, precise scientific instrumentation, restrained futuristic portal-ring geometry. **DPN Technology:** Develop · Pioneer · Navigate. Professional R&D, not a fictional functioning machine.

| Token | Color | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#0C1223` | background |
| Deep panel | `#182441` | UI surfaces |
| DPN violet | `#9B73FA` | design & engineering |
| Signal cyan | `#45D7ED` | evidence & measurements |
| Soft white | `#ECF4FF` | primary text |
| Research amber | `#F2AF79` | **unverified** scientific claims |
| Muted steel | `#87A8C9` | secondary labels |

## Visual behavior
- The PhaseGate emblem is a conceptual two-way aperture ring, **not a photograph of a real device**.
- Avoid CGI that suggests operating teleportation equipment. Always label illustrations as conceptual.
- Diagram arrows reflect **data flow only**; no arrows from A to B may imply matter transfer in the PG-01 design.
- Respect clear title hierarchy, short explanations, visual navigation and meaningful alt-text.
- All SVG files must remain local, script-free, vendor-independent and readable without JavaScript.
- Keep project-status labels evidence-based; review them when milestones change.
